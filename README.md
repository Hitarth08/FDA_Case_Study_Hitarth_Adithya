About

This is a case study of the Satyam Computer Services accounting fraud, which came to light in January 2009 and is widely called "India's Enron". The document explains the fraud in simple terms, how it was carried out, why it went undetected, its impact on different groups of people, and the legal action and reforms that followed.

The case study is about 3-4 pages long.

Key facts at a glance
Company: Satyam Computer Services, Hyderabad, India
Founder and chairman: B. Ramalinga Raju
Confession: 7 January 2009
Size of fraud: about Rs 7,000 crore (around US$1.5 billion) of inflated assets
Auditor: PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC)
Rescue: Tech Mahindra took over in 2009; the company merged into Tech Mahindra in 2013
